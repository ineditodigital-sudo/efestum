# Naming History

EFESTUM remained preferred because the conceptual foundation was strongest, although memorability was a concern.

Alternatives explored: BRONTUM, TEMPRUM, EFERUM, FERVUM, TESSUM, ERGIUM, TRAMUM, LIGIUM and other near-Efestum routes.

Feedback to preserve:
- Too many -UM endings felt repetitive.
- FEST strongly evokes fiesta/festivity in Mexico.
- Efra/Hefra feels like informal Efraín nickname.
- Eferum lacks phonetic punch.
- Hefto feels informal/alien/toy-like.
- Efor/Hefor lacks force.
- Festor resembles Néstor and foregrounds FEST.
- Several other names felt pharmaceutical, hard to spell or too detached from Hefesto/forja.

Do not treat any availability or trademark note here as current legal verification.
